## 1. Land Bank: market-tomato budget

**Contact:** `Agrieconadvisory@landbank.co.za`, given in the

**Subject:** Source and reuse questions about the 2025/26 market-tomato budget

Dear Agricultural Economics and Advisory team,

We are documenting possible inputs for a future retrospective crop-decision
evaluation. We found the “Tomatoes Market” one-hectare budget in your 2025/26
Vegetable Enterprise Budget. Could you please confirm:

1. Its first public release date, revision/version history, and the period and
   locality represented by its costs and 50 t/ha target yield?
2. Whether “Tomatoes Market” means fresh-market table tomatoes, which grades and
   sales channel it assumes, and whether the example R9,000/t price is farm-gate,
   wholesale-market or another basis?
3. Whether the R347,315.55/ha total allocatable variable costs and 12.5%
   marketing rate are inclusive or exclusive of VAT, and whether transport,
   packaging and market commission are included elsewhere in those figures?
4. Whether we may publish attributed normalized numeric fields, source hashes and
   derived comparison tables in an open-source repository, without redistributing
   the PDF; if permission is needed, what terms apply?

We can provide the URL and SHA-256 of the exact file we inspected. We will not
substitute these figures into our published result without a separately registered
protocol and a new evaluation.
