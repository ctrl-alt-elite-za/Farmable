The tomato section says **“Tomatoes Market”** and budgets one hectare at 50 t/ha.
It lists R9,000/t as its example market price, 12.5% marketing costs, and
R347,315.55/ha total allocatable variable costs. The displayed direct and
indirect variable subtotals are R332,000.07 and R15,315.48, respectively. At the
source's example price, the check is
`50 × 9,000 × (1 − 0.125) − 347,315.55 = R46,434.45/ha`, matching its displayed
gross margin above total allocatable variable costs. Packaging appears in the
variable costs; marketing is a separate percentage of gross income. The source
does not state a Western Cape locality, a planting calendar, or a VAT basis for
comparison with the existing Elsenburg budgets.

This is a closer **crop and market type candidate** than the frozen Elsenburg
processing-tomato budget, which has 0% marketing and is paired with fresh-market
Joburg prices in version 1. It is not a drop-in correction: its PDF was created
in 2026, its 2025/26 cost basis has not been reconciled to constant 2025 rand,
and its yield, cost and marketing assumptions differ from the registered rule.
The Land Bank example price is a budget assumption, not a replacement for the
audited Joburg monthly observations. A future evaluation must register the source
version, price/cost basis, yield and marketing mapping **before** running decisions;
it must preserve the published version 1 artifacts and report both rule sets.
