Foundations

Colour roles, type, spacing, radii, elevation and motion for Farmable. Every contrast ratio on this page is computed in your browser from the live token values using the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula — nothing here is eyeballed or transcribed.

Colour roles — light

Material 3 role names. The substrate is warm paper rather than white: outdoors, a pure-white surface in direct sun is the brightest thing in the farmer's field of view and forces a squint. Paper drops roughly 4% luminance without touching legibility.

Colour roles — dark

The dark page is pitch black; the surfaces on it are not. They stay warm charcoal and float, which is what carries depth now that shadows are invisible against #000. Black also costs no backlight on the OLED panels these devices increasingly ship, which matters to someone rationing charge on a farm.

Status and connectivity — the two ramps that must not touch

status/needsAttention is amber (hue ≈ 40°) and connectivity/offline is a neutral slate (hue ≈ 210°, chroma near zero). Neither is reachable from the red ramp, so "the farmer is offline" cannot be rendered as an error by accident. Offline is a normal operating state for this user, not a fault. Every status below carries an icon and a word as well as a colour.

Computed contrast — WCAG 2.1 relative luminance

Threshold is 4.5:1 for every pair in both themes, including large text and icon-bearing status chips, because this product is read in direct sunlight on a cheap LCD panel.

ThemeForegroundBackgroundHexRatio4.5:1

Type — San Francisco (system stack)

The app face is San Francisco, wired as a system-font stack. On iOS and macOS it resolves to SF from the OS's own copy; on Android — the target device — it resolves to Roboto; elsewhere to Inter, the closest freely-licensed face to SF. Floor: 14px body, 13px meta.

SF Pro is not bundled and cannot be. Apple's Font Licence forbids embedding it in software, and forbids using it even to design mock-ups for a non-Apple OS — and this product ships on Android. No SF files are in this repo. Asking the OS for its own UI font is a different thing and is fine, which is what the stack does.

So Sipho never sees San Francisco. Use the System / Android switch in the top bar to see what his Galaxy A13 actually renders. If the SF look matters on the device that matters, the answer is to bundle Inter.

Spacing — 4px base

Radii

Large radii are inherited from the reference. They also serve the target device: a 28px corner reads as a distinct object at arm's length in sun, where a 4px corner does not.

Elevation

Five levels, all single-shadow and low-opacity. Depth comes from surface tint plus one diffuse shadow.

Glass

Three blur strengths and nothing outside them. Scroll the strip below — the glass surfaces sit over moving content, which is the point. Every glass fill is high-alpha because the contrast check composites it over both black and white and demands 4.5:1 at both extremes; the first run failed three times and the alphas were raised until it passed. Each surface also carries a @supports not (backdrop-filter) fallback to solid, so nothing is legible only because of the blur.

Motion

Tap a tile to play the duration. All motion collapses to 1ms under prefers-reduced-motion.

Icons

Lucide (ISC licence), 24×24 grid, 2px stroke, inlined into farmable.js rather than loaded from a CDN — the mockup must open with no network, for the same reason the product must run with no network. In Flutter these map to lucide_icons or hand-exported SVG assets. An icon never appears without a word next to it in a core action.
