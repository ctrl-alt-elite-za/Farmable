Farmable

Design direction v1 — Sipho Dlamini · Siyakhula Farm · KwaMashu

A visual design system for a South African smallholder farming app, built for an entry-level Android phone used outdoors, often offline, by someone who would rather speak and point a camera than fill in a form. Open any page below; there is no build step, and the only network request is the two Google fonts — every icon, farm scene, map and camera frame is generated inline.

What this direction commits to

Carried forward as requirements
  • Contrast computed, not eyeballed — 82 solid + 48 glass composite pairs, 0 failures.
  • status/needsAttention is amber; connectivity/offline is a neutral slate. Neither is reachable from the red ramp.
  • San Francisco as the app face, via a system stack — SF on Apple, Roboto on the target Android device, Inter elsewhere. No font files shipped.
  • 14px body floor, 13px meta floor.
Taken from the reference template
  • Warm paper-stone substrate instead of white.
  • Very large radii, and the card-as-mat framing around every image.
  • Pill language: chips, pill buttons, circular icon buttons.
  • A dark ink surface as a counterpoint rather than a second theme.
  • Pitch-black dark mode, with warm charcoal surfaces floating on it.
  • Backdrop blur, on three fixed strengths, and the floating pill nav — brought down to phone scale as a bottom island.
Deliberately rejected from it
  • Its neutral text tones (stone-400 on white is 2.9:1) — replaced with a computed ramp.
  • Amber as the brand accent: amber is claimed by status/needsAttention, so the accent moved to a deep agricultural green.
  • Bento density — a dashboard grid is the wrong instinct for this user.
